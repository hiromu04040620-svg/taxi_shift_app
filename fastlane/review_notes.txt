The non-consumable In-App Purchase is implemented with StoreKit and is available without signing in.

Product ID: remove_ads
Display name: 広告非表示

Purchase steps:
1. Launch TaxiShift from the Home Screen.
2. Tap the ad area at the bottom of the Calendar screen, or open Settings and tap "広告非表示".
3. On the Premium screen, confirm that the App Store price is displayed.
4. Tap the purchase button and complete the Sandbox purchase.
5. The ads are removed after the transaction completes. "購入を復元" is available on the same screen.

The attached physical-device recording begins on the iOS Home Screen and demonstrates the calendar, daily entry, summary, and successful Sandbox purchase flow.
