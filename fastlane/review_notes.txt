This build implements Google Mobile Ads banners and the non-consumable In-App Purchase with StoreKit. The app is fully available without signing in.

Product ID: remove_ads
Display name: 広告非表示

Advertising and privacy:
- Banner ads appear only at the bottom of the Calendar and Summary screens.
- App Review, TestFlight, Sandbox receipts, and local builds use Google's official iOS test banner ad unit. App Store production receipts use the production banner ad unit.
- The app updates UMP consent information before requesting ads and shows any required regional consent form.
- On iOS, the ATT request is shown only when authorization is not determined. Denying ATT does not restrict any app feature.
- When required by UMP, users can reopen "広告のプライバシー設定" from Settings.

Purchase steps:
1. Launch TaxiShift from the Home Screen.
2. Confirm the Google test banner at the bottom of the Calendar or Summary screen.
3. Open Settings and tap "広告非表示".
4. On the Premium screen, confirm that the App Store price is displayed.
5. Tap "広告非表示を購入" and complete the Sandbox purchase.
6. Return to the Calendar and Summary screens. The banner and its space disappear immediately after the transaction completes.
7. "購入を復元" is available on the same Premium screen.
